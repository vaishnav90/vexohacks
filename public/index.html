Relay is a voice-controlled wearable that helps EMS crews coordinate care without reaching for a screen. Medics assign tasks out loud, the right teammate's band lights up, and Relay tracks each task until it's reported done.

One sentence in. The right band lights up.

Relay turns what the crew says into tasks with an owner and a status. Not just a transcript: every request is tracked from assignment to acceptance to a reported result.

Pick a step to see what Relay does.

Assign by voice

“Assign repeat blood pressure to Vaishnav.” Relay works out what the task is, puts it on the board, and confirms it out loud through MiniMax.

Private band alerts

Only the assignee's band lights up, with a colour that means something: orange for a new task, red for a timer, purple when Relay needs you to repeat.

Tracked to done

A double-tap on the band accepts the task. The reported result is linked back to the request, so the board shows what's pending, in progress, and reported done.

How it works

From voice to band in five steps

  1. 01SpeakThe coordinator talks naturally into the laptop mic or band.
  2. 02DeepgramTurns speech into text in real time.
  3. 03OpenAIReads the sentence and picks one action: assign a task, send it to a teammate, report a result, flag a timer, or ask to repeat.
  4. 04Relay serverKeeps the task list in code, never in the AI model, and decides what happens.
  5. 05Band + voiceThe assignee's band lights up and MiniMax speaks a short confirmation.

Relay only says what actually happened: every spoken confirmation is built from the task state, not written by the AI.
